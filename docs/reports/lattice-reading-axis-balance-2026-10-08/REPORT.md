# Axis-balanced lattice reading selection (synthetic only)

The per-frame selector now visits measured, eligible `(axis, side)` buckets round-robin, taking at most one reading from each bucket per round. Each bucket retains its prior confidence, polygon and stable region-ID order. Ineligible retained readings get a turn only after eligible buckets. The final returned tuple retains the existing canonical `(axis, side, -confidence, polygon, region ID)` order. Measurement, structural eligibility (`MIN_STRUCTURAL_SHARE=0.15`), region/phase evidence, six-reading cap and two-per-`(axis, side)` cap are unchanged.

A controlled crowded synthetic proposal set has three ranked candidates in each of six axis/side buckets. The prior axis-major selector could take four axis0 and two axis1 readings, leaving eligible axis2 unused. The new selector returns one from each bucket. Reversing proposal input preserves the selected reading content and evidence identity. An axis0-only control returns four readings and invents no other axis. An ineligible axis0 reading cannot displace an eligible axis1 reading at cap one. The scalar-observer control closes all 24 expected candidate identities across crowded and absent-axis frames: 10 accepted, 12 `reading_cap`, two `per_axis_side_cap`; zero unclassified, unexpected, multiply classified or omitted events. Every selected reading's observer canonical index, source region mask and phase source ID bind to its exact proposal.
