Concurrent findings remain `unresolved`. Capacity sufficiency is an upper bound, not proof that a homography exists.

Replay also checks true proposal and accepted-support aggregates against sealed quota-v2 per-pair metrics, rejecting consistently resealed truth edits that change those aggregates.
