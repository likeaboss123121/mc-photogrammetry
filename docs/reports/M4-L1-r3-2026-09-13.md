## L1B audit correction (2026-09-13)

R3 is retained as immutable pixels-only output but is INVALID for L1 acceptance. A direct trace found that plane_window used the refined principal point saved in the reading, while RGB and mask rectification in _bounded_measure and edge-energy rectification in _measure_plane_candidates omitted it and silently used image center. Frame 07 reading 3 reproduces the discrepancy: with the omission the rounds are 392→7.38098, 531→9.35990, 567→5.52197; with the saved principal the first round is 392→4.27152 and the next request is 918. The 9/32 replay mismatch is therefore a production dataflow defect, not an unresolved diagnostic/parity issue. No R3 rerun is authorized. L1B preregisters principal propagation and off-center synthetic coverage, then one new R4 only.
